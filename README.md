# Undercover / Mr. White Party Game 🕵️‍♂️

A sleek, mobile-responsive "Pass & Play" web application for the popular party game **Undercover** (also known as Mr. White). Built with vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

## 🚀 Play Now
**[Live Demo on GitHub Pages](https://sqiprasanna.github.io/mr-white-undercover/)**

---

## 🎮 How to Play (Pass & Play)

This version is optimized for in-person gatherings where players pass a single phone around.

1.  **Setup**: 
    *   Enter the names of all players (4–15 players).
    *   Set the number of **Undercovers** and **Mr. Whites**.
    *   Click **Start Game**.
2.  **Secret Reveal**:
    *   The "Who are you?" screen appears.
    *   One by one, each player finds their name, taps it, and reveals their secret card.
    *   **Tap the card** to flip it and see your word (Civilians/Undercover) or role (Mr. White).
    *   Click **"I've seen it"** to hide your role and pass the phone to the next person.
3.  **The Game**:
    *   Once everyone is marked with a ✅, the Host clicks **Start Discussion**.
    *   **Civilians**: Describe your word without being too obvious.
    *   **Undercover**: You have a slightly different word. Try to blend in!
    *   **Mr. White**: You have no word. Listen carefully and guess the word to survive.
4.  **Elimination**:
    *   After each round of descriptions, vote to eliminate someone.
    *   The Host marks the player as eliminated on the **Game Board**.
5.  **Winning**:
    *   **Civilians** win if they eliminate all Undercovers and Mr. Whites.
    *   **Undercovers/Mr. White** win if they outnumber the Civilians.

## ✨ Features
- **200+ Word Pairs**: High replayability with autogenerated word sets.
- **Privacy First**: Cards are hidden until tapped, and "Seen" status is tracked.
- **Offline Ready**: No backend or database required.
- **Mobile First**: Designed to look and feel like a native app on your smartphone browser.

---

## 🛠️ Technology
- **Vanilla JavaScript**: Lightweight and fast.
- **CSS3 Transitions**: Smooth card flipping animations.
- **GitHub Pages**: Free static hosting.
- **RTK-AI**: Optimized for AI-driven development.
